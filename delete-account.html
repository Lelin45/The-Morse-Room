Deleting your account permanently removes your chat profile, sign-in records, friendships, requests, memberships, blocked-person records, and messages you sent. Your direct conversations are also removed. Related safety reports are removed.

Removal from the live database happens when deletion completes. Cloudflare's automatic database recovery history can contain earlier records for up to seven days. Copies saved by people who received your messages cannot be removed by this service.
