# Upload order in Google Play Console

1. Open your app, then Store presence and Main store listing.
2. Copy `app-name.txt`, `short-description.txt` and `full-description.txt` into their matching fields.
3. Upload `icon.png` as the app icon and `feature-graphic.png` as the feature graphic.
4. Choose the plain screenshots in `screenshots/phone` or the promotional Android phone cards in `screenshots/phone-framed`. Upload the eight PNGs from your chosen folder in filename order. Do not combine both sets into one phone listing.
5. Upload the PNGs from `screenshots/tablet-7` to the 7-inch tablet section.
6. Upload the PNGs from `screenshots/tablet-10` to the 10-inch tablet section.
7. The video field accepts a YouTube URL. Upload the MP4 from `video` to your own YouTube channel as Public or Unlisted, turn off ads, and leave age restrictions off. Paste that video's URL. You can leave this optional field empty until then.
8. Save the listing. All screenshots and promotional cards already have the required dimensions; upload them without resizing.

The file `asset-manifest.json` records the dimensions, file sizes and description lengths. `image-descriptions.txt` provides short alt text for each image.

For the Privacy policy field, use:

https://learnmorse.lelinadhikari.com.np/privacy.html

For the Account deletion URL, use:

https://learnmorse.lelinadhikari.com.np/delete-account.html

Learn, Practice and Codes work without sign-in. For restricted app access, reviewers can open Chat and tap Review access. Use the review credentials you entered privately in Play Console.

The AAB belongs in a release track, such as Internal testing. The APK is for installing and testing the app yourself. Neither belongs in the screenshot or graphic fields.
