Morse Chat Room terms

Morse Room offers free receiving lessons and practice. Chat is a place to practise Morse through conversations with friends and private groups.

Be a good conversation partner

Use your own sign-in account and choose a username that does not impersonate another person. Do not send spam, harassment, threats, or illegal content. Respect other people's choice to decline a friend request.

Private group invitations

People who have a group's invite code or link can join and read its message history. Keep invitations within your intended circle. Send messages you are comfortable sharing with every member of that conversation.

Messages and availability

Morse display and audio use only letters A-Z and numbers 0-9. Punctuation and symbols are skipped in the signal. Show text displays the original message, including punctuation, spacing, and new lines. Messages must include at least one letter or number. The text can be revealed by conversation members. Chat depends on an internet connection and may be interrupted or temporarily unavailable.

The site owner may remove abusive accounts or content. To raise a concern, contact the owner through lelinadhikari.com.np. Read the privacy notice for how chat data is stored.
